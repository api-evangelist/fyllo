# Semasio

> Semasio is a Hamburg, Germany advertising-technology company whose semantic targeting
> platform builds audience, contextual and brand-fit segments for brands, agencies and DSPs
> across roughly 30 languages and 50 countries. Founded in 2010, acquired in 2022 by Casters
> Holdings, Inc. (Fyllo), rebranded to Semasio in April 2024, and acquired by Samba TV on
> 31 October 2024.

**Semasio publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API reference, no SDK,
no webhook catalog and no machine-readable specification (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL,
Postman collection or JSON Schema) on any Semasio host. An agent cannot integrate with
Semasio programmatically from public information; access runs through a sales conversation
and the login-only Semasio platform.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from the public profile at
https://apis.io/provider/fyllo — it is not published by Semasio.

## Products

- [Audience Targeting](https://www.semasio.com/products/audience-targeting): Pre-built and custom audience segments.
- [Audience Extension](https://www.semasio.com/products/audience-extension): Model and extend reach from a seed audience.
- [Contextual Targeting](https://www.semasio.com/products/contextual-targeting): Cookie-free, page-level semantic targeting.
- [Contextual Audience Extension](https://www.semasio.com/products/contextual-audience-extension): ContextualPLUS — predictive combination of audience and context from a small seed set.
- [Brand Fit](https://www.semasio.com/products/brand-fit): Brand-safety and brand-suitability alignment.
- [Interactive Screens](https://www.semasio.com/products/interactive-screens): Personalized on-screen customer journeys.
- [Semasio Factory](https://www.semasio.com/factory/factory): Data onboarding, modeling and distribution service.
- [Media Strategy & Activation](https://www.semasio.com/services/media-strategy-activation): Managed media planning and activation.
